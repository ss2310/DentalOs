# GrowthOS — Demo Scripts & Headlines

Three scripts: the 90-second hook, the 10-minute standard demo, and the
30-minute deep dive. Same spine, different depth. Demo on a phone (or a
narrow browser window) — the audience runs their clinic from a phone, and the
app is built phone-first.

**Golden rules**
1. Demo with REAL local data: seed the demo clinic with the prospect's actual
   area/city before the call. The Map Rank heatmap and Deep Audit hit 10×
   harder on their own neighbourhood.
2. Never say "AI will post for you" — say "nothing goes out without your
   thumb on the button." Doctors fear losing control; control IS the pitch.
3. End every section on a ₹ number or a WhatsApp send — the two things a
   clinic owner feels.

## Major headlines (pick per audience)

**Master headline**
> **"Aapke clinic ka growth department — ₹2,499/month."**
> (Your clinic's growth department, for less than one consultation a day.)

**Money angle** — "Every clinic loses lakhs a year to no-shows, unpaid dues,
and patients who never come back. GrowthOS gets them back — and shows you the
₹ it recovered."

**Marketing angle** — "When someone asks ChatGPT for the best dentist in
{area}, is it you? We measure it, then run the 30-day plan to fix it."

**Control angle** — "AI writes, you approve, WhatsApp sends. No bots talk to
your patients — ever."

**Social angle** — "A month of branded Instagram, Facebook, and Google posts
— in your clinic's voice, with your logo — approved by you in 10 minutes a
week. Images free."

**Proof angle** — "Your happy patients are your best marketing. Capture the
smile chairside — with real consent — and it becomes a review request today
and a branded post tomorrow."

## Script A — the 90-second hook (walk-in / WhatsApp video / reel)

> [Open Dashboard] "This is a dental clinic's morning: 3 new enquiries, 2 dues
> to collect, 5 check-ups due. Each one is one tap on WhatsApp — in Hinglish,
> already written."
> [Tap a reminder → wa.me opens] "That's a patient coming back."
> [Open Map Rank heatmap] "This is where you rank on Google Maps around your
> clinic. Green means patients find you, red means they find your competitor."
> [Open Deep Audit report] "And this is the machine's honest report on your
> whole online presence — including whether ChatGPT recommends you — with a
> 30-day plan to fix it."
> "₹2,499 a month. Less than one consultation a day. Shall I run the audit on
> YOUR clinic?"

**The closer that books the next meeting:** run a Deep Audit on their clinic
live (₹100 of API cost to potentially close a ₹30k/year customer).

## Script B — the 10-minute standard demo

### 1. The morning (2 min) — Dashboard, Enquiries
- "Har subah receptionist yahan se shuru karti hai." Walk the to-do list.
- Open an enquiry → one-tap WhatsApp reply → point at "✓ Sent".
- **Line:** "Nobody falls through the cracks — the app remembers so your
  staff doesn't have to."

### 2. The money (2 min) — Treatment Plans, Payments, Recalls
- Treatment Plans: "₹1.4 lakh sitting in 'thinking about it' — each card is
  one follow-up tap."
- Recalls: 6-month checkup dues with ready Hinglish reminders.
- Flash Revenue Recovered: "The app counts every rupee it brings back. This
  is your ROI meter."

### 3. The reputation (2 min) — Reviews + Moments
- Post-visit survey flow: "Happy patients get pushed to Google. Unhappy ones
  come to YOU privately — before they write it publicly."
- Moments: snap → two consent toggles → review ask. **Line:** "Consent
  recorded, photo private, one review request per patient per 30 days — we
  built the rules in so you can't accidentally break them."

### 4. The growth engine (3 min) — Social, Map Rank
- Social weekly planner: answer the 3 questions live → 5 posts appear in the
  queue → open one: branded image with THEIR logo, natural Hinglish caption.
- Approve one → publish screen: copy, download, share. **Line:** "Ten minutes
  on Monday, and your clinic posts all week. Nothing goes out without you."
- Map Rank heatmap on their keyword. Pause. Let the red sink in.

### 5. The close (1 min) — Deep Audit
- Open the prepared report: score vs top rival, the AI-visibility verdict,
  the 30-day plan. **Line:** "This is the plan a ₹50,000 agency would sell
  you. It's included. Shall we start your trial today — first audit free?"

## Script C — the 30-minute deep dive (serious buyer / dental chain)

Sections 1–5 as above, slower, plus:

6. **Voice Notes** (if flagged on): dictate a note live → show the staged
   extraction → "AI proposes, your staff confirms. It cannot message anyone."
7. **Brand Personality wizard**: run 2 screens live — "This is why the posts
   sound like YOUR clinic, not a template. Claims without a source can never
   appear in a post."
8. **Campaigns**: build a "6-months lapsed" segment → AI-drafted broadcast.
9. **Data migration**: "Bring your old patients in with one CSV — AI maps the
   columns."
10. **Staff & roles**: receptionist view vs owner view (Marketing hidden).
11. **Recurring engine**: "Every 30 days the audit re-runs, and you get a
    'what moved' digest — including when a competitor's reviews start growing
    3× faster than yours."
12. **Objection block** (keep answers ready):
    - *"WhatsApp ban ho jayega?"* — "Everything sends from YOUR WhatsApp, one
      tap at a time, to YOUR patients. No bulk API, no bot."
    - *"Data kahan hai?"* — "Isolated per clinic at the database level;
      patient photos private with recorded consent; you can delete anything."
    - *"AI galat cheez likh dega?"* — show the YMYL validator catching a fake
      "95% success" claim live (type it into a caption edit).
    - *"Mere paas time nahi hai"* — "Weekly planner: 3 questions, Monday, 10
      minutes."

## Explainer videos — recommendation

Don't produce per-page explainer videos before launch; features are still
moving and videos rot fastest. Ship with:
1. ONE 6–8 min "full tour" video (Script B verbatim — it is the storyboard).
2. The in-app help chatbot (page-aware, Hinglish) as the per-page explainer.
3. After 10+ real clinics: record 60–90s Hinglish clips for the 4 screens
   where users actually get stuck (watch support questions to pick them),
   Loom-style over a phone screen recording.

Every script above doubles as the voiceover script when that time comes.
