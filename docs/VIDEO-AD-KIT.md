# Video Ad Kit — snappy ad → landing page → free trial

The PAID funnel, distinct from `FB-AUDIT-VIDEO.md` (that one is the organic
"DM me" post): a 30-second ad stops the scroll and sends clicks to
**themaskedmarketers.com**, where a calmer 2-minute tour video does the
convincing and the "Start free trial" button closes. Different CTA, different
pacing — but **one recording session covers both kits** (shot list below).

```
30s snappy ad (Reels/Shorts/FB feed)  →  click  →  themaskedmarketers.com
                                                    ├─ 2-min tour video
                                                    └─ Start free trial → app.themaskedmarketers.com/signup
```

## ⚠️ Same two recording rules as the FB kit
Record on the DEMO clinic only (never a real clinic's data), no patient info
in frame. The demo clinic from `docs/MANUAL-TEST-SCRIPT.md` has everything
these scripts need.

## Script 1 — the snappy ad (~32s, vertical 1080×1920 master)

Paid-ad rules: the hook must land **inside 2 seconds**, every shot ≤5s, hard
cuts on the beat, captions always (85% watch muted), CTA twice (mid-roll
overlay + end card). VO in energetic Hinglish.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover | Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | ChatGPT answering "best dentist in Jaipur" — other clinics listed | "ChatGPT se poocho — aapka clinic list mein hai?" | **Aapka naam nahi aaya? 😬** |
| 2–6s | Phone: dashboard loaded with the demo day | "Ye hai aapke clinic ka poora growth department — ek app." | **GrowthOS 🦷** |
| 6–11s | Map Rank heatmap, punch-in on a red pin turning the corner of the grid | "Google Maps par aapki ASLI ranking — pin by pin." | **Har pin = asli data** |
| 11–16s | Social planner: 3 questions → 5 posts fill the queue → branded image renders with logo | "3 sawaal — poora hafta social media. Aapke logo ke saath. Images free." | **3 sawaal = 7 din ka content** |
| 16–21s | The ✨ moment: same post — tap "Photo backdrop" → flat card transforms into the studio-photo version | "Aur ek tap mein — magazine-level visuals." | **✨ Ek tap. Dekha?** |
| 21–26s | Deep Audit report scroll: score vs rival → 30-day plan | "8 minute ka AI audit — wahi report jo agency ₹50,000 mein bechti hai." | **₹50,000 report. Included.** |
| 26–32s | End card: tooth mark + URL | "30 din free. No card. Link par tap karo." | **30 din FREE → themaskedmarketers.com** |

### Three hook variants (A/B/C test — swap only the first 2s + line 1)
- **A. The AI fear (above):** ChatGPT listing competitors.
- **B. The red map:** heatmap full of red pins → "Ye red pins? Har ek ek
  patient hai jo aapke competitor ke paas gaya."
- **C. The time burn:** timelapse of someone typing an Instagram caption at
  11 PM → "Raat ko captions likh rahe ho? Doctor ho ya social media manager?"

Meta lets you A/B hooks on one ad set — run all three for 3–4 days, kill the
losers, scale the winner.

## Wiring the tour video into the landing page

`marketing-site/index.html` has a ready **commented-out video section** right
under the hero (search for `VIDEO SECTION`). When the tour video is done:
1. Upload it to YouTube as **Unlisted** (free hosting, fast player).
2. Replace `YOUTUBE_VIDEO_ID` in that block with the ID from the URL.
3. Un-comment the block, re-drop the folder on Netlify. Done — the hero
   also gains a "▶ Watch the 2-minute tour" link automatically (same block).

## Running the ad (quick notes)

- **Platform:** Meta (FB + IG) first — Indian dentists are on both; Reels
  placement + feed. Budget ₹300–500/day to start.
- **Targeting:** interests "dentistry/dental practice" + job titles, age
  27–55, metro + tier-2 cities; or a 1%-lookalike once you have 50 signups.
- **Objective:** Traffic to themaskedmarketers.com at first; switch to
  Conversions once the Netlify page has ~50 clicks (add Meta Pixel later).
- **Measure:** trial signups per city per hook. The audit hook (A) should
  win; if (C) wins, your buyers are pain-driven — lead demos with Social.
