## ⚠️ Before you record — two rules

1. **Record everything on your DEMO clinic** (built by
   `docs/DEMO-DAY-TEST-PLAN.md`), or a friend's clinic WITH permission.
   Never post a real clinic's competitive data uninvited — the clinic and
   every named competitor are future customers.
2. No patient data in frame. Use the dummy patients (they're all your own
   phone number anyway). Check the notification bell before recording.
