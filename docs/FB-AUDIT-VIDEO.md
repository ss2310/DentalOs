# The Deep Audit Demo Video — script, shot list, caption, Fiverr brief

One 60-second vertical video (1080×1920) for Facebook/Instagram/WhatsApp
Status. Built entirely from screen recordings of the app — no camera needed
(though a 5-second selfie clip at the end converts better).

## ⚠️ Before you record — two rules

1. **Run the audit on your DEMO clinic, or on a friend's clinic WITH his
   permission** (best version: "I ran this on Dr. Rahul's clinic — with his
   blessing"). Never publicly post a real clinic's competitive teardown
   uninvited — the clinic AND the named competitors are all potential
   customers you'd burn.
2. No patient data anywhere in frame. The audit report has none, but check
   the notification bell / other tabs before recording.

---

## The concept

Hook with the one question every doctor secretly worries about
("When someone asks ChatGPT for the best dentist in your area — does your
name come up?") → show the machine working → show the verdict → show the
plan → CTA. The "they doubted me" energy goes in the CAPTION, one line,
classy — the video itself stays 100% product confidence.

## Shot list (record these 7 clips, 15–30s each, trim in edit)

Use the demo clinic from `docs/DEMO-DAY-TEST-PLAN.md` (already populated).
Record on desktop at a narrow window OR on the phone (phone = more authentic
for FB). Android: built-in screen recorder; hide the status bar clutter.

1. ChatGPT (or Perplexity) being asked "best dentist in {area}" — capture the
   answer listing clinics.
2. /audit → the Run tap → 10s of the stage progress labels animating.

4. AI-visibility panel (the engine-by-engine verdict).
5. /rank heatmap — zoom/hover a green and a red pin.
6. The 30-day plan — steady scroll, expand one item so the evidence shows.
7. (Optional) 5-second selfie: you, phone in hand, one line to camera.

## The DM-to-customer pipeline (don't skip this)

The post's real product is the DM list. For every DM:
1. Same day: run their audit (₹100 API cost — your best CAC ever).
2. Send 2–3 report screenshots, NOT the link ("full report call par").
3. 15-min call = Demo Script B → end on the trial.
Track every DM name in Enquiries inside your own GrowthOS — eat your own
dog food and screenshot THAT for the follow-up post: "12 DMs in 2 days,
tracked in my own app. 😉"
