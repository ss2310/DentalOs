FAQ

Questions clinics actually ask.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

No — and that's deliberate. Every message opens in your own WhatsApp with the text pre-filled, sent from your clinic's number with one tap. No API approval process, no per-message fees, no risk to your number from bulk sending.

Will AI post things without my approval?

Never. Every post, reply, and message waits in an approval queue until you tap Approve. A built-in safety check also blocks made-up statistics, prices, or medical promises before they ever reach you.

What exactly is a credit?

One credit ≈ one short piece of AI content (a review reply, a WhatsApp broadcast, a social post). Bigger jobs cost more — a full SEO blog article is 3, a ✨ premium AI image is 1–2. Your plan includes 100 every month, and packs top you up from ₹549.

What happens when my trial ends?

Nothing scary — your data stays safe. Upgrade to Growth (₹2,999/month) to keep going; if you don't, the app pauses until you do. No card is taken during the trial, so nothing is charged automatically.

Is my patient data safe?

Yes. Every clinic's data is isolated at the database level (row-level security) — no other clinic can ever see your patients. Patient photos are stored privately and shared only with recorded consent.

My receptionist isn't technical. Will she manage?

It's built for the front desk first: big buttons, plain language, works on any Android phone, and the risky stuff (marketing, money, settings) is only visible to the owner and doctors.
